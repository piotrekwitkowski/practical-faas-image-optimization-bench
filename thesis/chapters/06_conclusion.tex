\section{Conclusion \& Future Work}
\label{cha:conclusion}

Finally, we would like to acknowledge that while we were able to deploy and analyze results from over 100 versions of essentially very similar image optimizing functions, we recognize that extending the parameter space further would likely introduce a level of combinatorial complexity that may present a logistical challenge. Ideally, a benchmark like ours should operate on continuous, rather than discrete, parameter values, allowing for a more granular exploration of the optimization landscape. However, the implementation of such a system would necessitate a sophisticated framework capable of managing and analyzing the resulting high-dimensional data, a task that surpasses the scope of this study.
