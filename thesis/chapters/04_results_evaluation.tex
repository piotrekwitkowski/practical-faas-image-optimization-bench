\section{Results}
\label{cha:results}

This chapter presents the results focusing on the optimization performance metrics observed during two month long optimization configuration and platforms.  Our primary goal is to understand the practical implications of FaaS performance variability in a real-world image optimization scenario.  We analyze several key performance indicators, including cold start duration, image processing duration, and implicitly, the impact of memory allocation through performance settings.  Furthermore, we investigate performance variability from different perspectives: general variability across all runs, temporal variability over time, variability across image formats, and variability across platform configurations.
